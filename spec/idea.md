# Recipe Information System and Dish Search by Available Ingredients

## Project idea

The project is an information system for storing and organizing cooking recipes and helping users find dishes that can be prepared from ingredients they currently have available.

The system is intended to allow users to browse recipes, view information about ingredients and preparation steps, and search for suitable dishes based on available ingredients.

Registered users will be able to add their own recipes and photos of prepared dishes and manage the recipes they have created.

The primary users of the system are registered users who want to store their own recipes and find dishes based on ingredients available at home.
The system may also support guests who can browse and search recipes.

The system will maintain structured information about recipes and ingredients so that recipes can be searched and matched according to the ingredients available to the user.

The detailed functional and non-functional requirements will be defined during the specification stage of the project.
